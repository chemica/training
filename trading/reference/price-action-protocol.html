Read left. Think up. Act down.

A candle is evidence of an auction over an interval—not a prediction. Interpret it through location, sequence, and timeframe.

1 · Context

4h — trend, range, or transition? Mark only obvious swing zones. Note nearby event risk.

2 · Setup

1h — what behaviour at what location would create an opportunity? Pullback, breakout, failure, or mean reversion?

3 · Trigger

5m/15m — what observable event permits entry? State the exact condition and the condition that kills the idea.

The four-line hypothesis

  1. Context: current regime and structural location.
  2. Setup: behaviour expected at that location.
  3. Trigger: observable event required before entry.
  4. Invalidation: observable evidence that the thesis is wrong.
Stop test: if invalidation is merely “my stop was hit”, the reasoning is backwards. Structure determines invalidation; invalidation plus risk budget determines size.

Scale traps

  • A 5-minute uptrend may be only a 4-hour bearish pullback.
  • A large candle says volatility expanded; it does not reveal what happens next.
  • Support and resistance are hypotheses about zones, not guaranteed lines.
  • More indicators do not create independent evidence when they are transformations of the same price series.
