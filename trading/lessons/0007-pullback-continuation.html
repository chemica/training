Lesson 0007 · 30 minutes · One decision

Validate a pullback continuation.

By the end: Decide whether a retracement qualifies for one written continuation playbook.

Boundary: all charts and tickets are historical, replayed or paper-only. Judge the decision made with information available then—not the eventual P&L.

0–5 min · The invariant

A pullback is a hypothesis inside established directional structure. Location, preserved structure, a resumption trigger and acceptable net asymmetry must all be present.

Impulse
Directional leg that makes structural progress.
Pullback
Counter-move that has not yet invalidated continuation.
Protected swing
Structural point whose loss damages the thesis.
Resumption trigger
Observable return of directional behaviour.

Open the shared glossary if a supporting term is unfamiliar.

5–18 min · One structure, five outcomes

Read each chart left to right. The paths are schematics, not predictions; compare the decisive evidence rather than memorising their silhouettes.

CanonicalExample 1

Impulse breaks a high; orderly retracement holds the protected low; trigger reclaims minor structure.

Decision: Eligible after the trigger.

Visual variationExample 2

Two large countertrend candles reach the zone but preserve the protected swing; resumption follows.

Decision: Ugly candles, same qualifying structure.

Opposite resolutionExample 3

Retracement accepts below the protected swing and rebound forms a lower high.

Decision: Continuation invalidated; possible transition or reversal.

Near missExample 4

Everything qualifies, but the next opposing zone leaves only 0.5R before costs.

Decision: Reject: valid pattern, invalid trade economics.

AmbiguousExample 5

Price holds the swing but overlaps at the zone without a resumption trigger.

Decision: Wait; location is not permission.

18–24 min · Unseen decision

Trend and location qualify. Price rallies from the zone before your written trigger and reaches the target area. Your process result?

24–30 min · Deliberate practice

Replay five pullback candidates. Before reveal, record regime, location, protected swing, trigger, invalidation and first obstacle. Include at least one rejection and keep rules fixed across all five.

Save one pre-outcome screenshot and your classification. Ask your LLM teacher: “Which observation decided the classification? What would falsify it? Ignore the eventual profit.”
