Lesson 0009 · 30 minutes · One decision

Qualify a range-boundary fade.

By the end: Decide whether a boundary interaction qualifies for mean reversion or whether expansion cancels it.

Boundary: all charts and tickets are historical, replayed or paper-only. Judge the decision made with information available then—not the eventual P&L.

0–5 min · The invariant

A range needs repeated two-sided response and rotation. A fade requires edge location, rejection evidence, structural invalidation and enough room after friction; acceptance outside kills it.

Range
Repeated two-sided auction between defensible boundaries.
Equilibrium
Interior area around which price repeatedly rotates.
Boundary fade
Conditional trade from an edge back toward equilibrium.
Expansion
Sustained movement away from the prior balance.

Open the shared glossary if a supporting term is unfamiliar.

5–18 min · One structure, five outcomes

Read each chart left to right. The paths are schematics, not predictions; compare the decisive evidence rather than memorising their silhouettes.

CanonicalExample 1

Third upper-boundary test rejects and lower-timeframe structure turns down.

Decision: Fade may qualify toward equilibrium.

Visual variationExample 2

Price overshoots the boundary deeply, then closes inside and fails to reclaim.

Decision: Different candles, same rejection sequence.

Opposite resolutionExample 3

Price closes beyond, holds outside and builds a higher low.

Decision: Acceptance cancels the fade; regime enters transition/expansion.

Near missExample 4

Rejection occurs, but entry is near the midpoint after a late chase.

Decision: Reject: poor location and compressed reward.

AmbiguousExample 5

A wick rejects but the next candles cluster at the boundary.

Decision: Unresolved; neither rotation nor acceptance is proven.

18–24 min · Unseen decision

A valid range is 100 bp wide. Estimated round-trip friction is 28 bp and your late entry leaves 35 bp to equilibrium. Decision?

24–30 min · Deliberate practice

Find five completed ranges. Freeze boundaries before the test. For each classify rejection, acceptance or unresolved, then decide take/wait/reject using location, invalidation and costs.

Save one pre-outcome screenshot and your classification. Ask your LLM teacher: “Which observation decided the classification? What would falsify it? Ignore the eventual profit.”
