Lesson 0002 · 30 minutes · One decision

Separate candle shape from intrabar path.

By the end: Given the same OHLC candle, describe multiple possible paths and state what cannot be inferred.

Boundary: all charts and tickets are historical, replayed or paper-only. Judge the decision made with information available then—not the eventual P&L.

0–5 min · The invariant

A candle preserves open, high, low and close for one interval. It discards ordering within the interval, spread, most trade-by-trade detail and the identities or intentions of participants.

Body
Distance between open and close.
Wick
Range beyond the body that traded during the interval.
Aggregation
Combining smaller intervals into one larger OHLC record.
Intrabar path
The ordered journey hidden inside an aggregated candle.

Open the shared glossary if a supporting term is unfamiliar.

5–18 min · One structure, five outcomes

Read each chart left to right. The paths are schematics, not predictions; compare the decisive evidence rather than memorising their silhouettes.

CanonicalExample 1

A green candle opens 100, trades 98, reaches 108 and closes 106.

Decision: It records four prices; it does not prove steady buying.

Visual variationExample 2

Another candle has identical OHLC but runs 100→108→98→106.

Decision: Same candle, radically different experience for intrabar traders.

Opposite resolutionExample 3

A long lower wick at support is followed by acceptance below support.

Decision: The wick proved rejection during one interval, not durable reversal.

Near missExample 4

A hammer-shaped candle forms in the centre of a broad range.

Decision: Shape exists; meaningful location and follow-through do not.

AmbiguousExample 5

A large-bodied candle closes at its high during a news burst.

Decision: Expansion is known; continuation, exhaustion and slippage remain unresolved.

18–24 min · Unseen decision

Two 1h candles have identical OHLC. Can you conclude that stop orders were triggered in the same sequence?

24–30 min · Deliberate practice

Find one 4h BTC candle. Inspect its sixteen 15m candles and sketch the path. Then hide the 15m view and write three claims the 4h candle supports and three it cannot support.

Save one pre-outcome screenshot and your classification. Ask your LLM teacher: “Which observation decided the classification? What would falsify it? Ignore the eventual profit.”
