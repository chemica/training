Lesson 0011 · 30 minutes · One decision

Choose an order by its failure mode.

By the end: Choose market, limit, stop-market or stop-limit for a paper setup and name the risk being accepted.

Boundary: all charts and tickets are historical, replayed or paper-only. Judge the decision made with information available then—not the eventual P&L.

0–5 min · The invariant

No order type guarantees both execution and price. Market orders prioritise immediacy; limits constrain price but may not fill; triggered orders inherit venue-specific activation and fill behaviour.

Market order
Immediate execution against available opposing liquidity.
Limit order
Execution only at the limit price or better; no fill guarantee.
Stop-market
After trigger, seeks execution with uncertain price.
Stop-limit
After trigger, limits price and may remain unfilled.

Open the shared glossary if a supporting term is unfamiliar.

5–18 min · One structure, five outcomes

Read each chart left to right. The paths are schematics, not predictions; compare the decisive evidence rather than memorising their silhouettes.

CanonicalExample 1

Breakout requires participation only after 50,000; a stop-market triggers there.

Decision: Accepts slippage to prioritise execution after evidence.

Visual variationExample 2

Pullback plan uses a limit at the zone, but price turns one tick early.

Decision: No fill is the accepted failure mode; do not chase automatically.

Opposite resolutionExample 3

Stop-market triggers into a gap and fills far beyond the expected price.

Decision: Execution occurred; price certainty failed.

Near missExample 4

Stop-limit is chosen because it sounds safer, with no plan if it triggers unfilled.

Decision: Incomplete ticket: price protection created execution risk.

AmbiguousExample 5

Displayed depth supports a limit order, but a catalyst is seconds away.

Decision: Queue, cancellation and adverse-selection risks are unresolved; waiting may dominate.

18–24 min · Unseen decision

You must exit if an invalidation trades during a fast move. Which trade-off best describes stop-limit versus stop-market?

24–30 min · Deliberate practice

For one pullback and one breakout, draft market, limit and triggered paper tickets. For each state expected fill, worst plausible fill, non-fill consequence, fee class and cancellation rule. Select one and justify its failure mode.

Save one pre-outcome screenshot and your classification. Ask your LLM teacher: “Which observation decided the classification? What would falsify it? Ignore the eventual profit.”
