Lesson 0003 · 30 minutes · One decision

Classify structure from meaningful swings.

By the end: Classify a window as trend, range or transition using a consistent swing threshold.

Boundary: all charts and tickets are historical, replayed or paper-only. Judge the decision made with information available then—not the eventual P&L.

0–5 min · The invariant

Structure is a sequence of meaningful pivots. The latest possible pivot is provisional; one break damages the old sequence but does not automatically establish the opposite trend.

Pivot
A local turning point confirmed by movement away.
Swing
A pivot meaningful at the selected scale.
Trend
Progression of meaningful highs and lows.
Transition
Old structure is damaged; a stable replacement is not established.

Open the shared glossary if a supporting term is unfamiliar.

5–18 min · One structure, five outcomes

Read each chart left to right. The paths are schematics, not predictions; compare the decisive evidence rather than memorising their silhouettes.

CanonicalExample 1

Higher high, higher low, then another higher high.

Decision: Uptrend at this scale.

Visual variationExample 2

Deep, volatile pullback remains above the protected swing, then a new high forms.

Decision: Still an uptrend; neat candles are not required.

Opposite resolutionExample 3

The protected low breaks; rebound forms a lower high; price makes another low.

Decision: Downtrend becomes established after transition.

Near missExample 4

One marginal lower low appears, then price immediately reclaims and makes a new high.

Decision: Damage or liquidity probe—not enough alone to label a downtrend.

AmbiguousExample 5

Highs compress while lows rise; neither directional sequence completes.

Decision: Transition/compression. Do not force a trend label.

18–24 min · Unseen decision

An uptrend breaks its last meaningful higher low, then rebounds strongly but has not formed a lower high or new high. Classification?

24–30 min · Deliberate practice

Hide the final 20 bars on five random 1h BTC windows. Mark at most six meaningful swings, classify the state, reveal five bars, and note whether your threshold—not the latest candle—drove any update.

Save one pre-outcome screenshot and your classification. Ask your LLM teacher: “Which observation decided the classification? What would falsify it? Ignore the eventual profit.”
