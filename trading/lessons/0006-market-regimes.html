Lesson 0006 · 30 minutes · One decision

Select a regime—or no trade.

By the end: Classify structure and volatility separately, then permit only a compatible playbook.

Boundary: all charts and tickets are historical, replayed or paper-only. Judge the decision made with information available then—not the eventual P&L.

0–5 min · The invariant

Directional versus rotational structure is one axis; quiet versus expanding volatility is another. “Unclear” is valid when observations conflict or a transition is incomplete.

Directional
Meaningful swings progress with follow-through.
Rotational
Price repeatedly returns through an area or range.
Volatility
Magnitude and speed of movement, separate from direction.
Unclear
Evidence does not support a stable classification.

Open the shared glossary if a supporting term is unfamiliar.

5–18 min · One structure, five outcomes

Read each chart left to right. The paths are schematics, not predictions; compare the decisive evidence rather than memorising their silhouettes.

CanonicalExample 1

Advancing swings and shallow pullbacks with ordinary volatility.

Decision: Directional/quiet; continuation playbook may be eligible.

Visual variationExample 2

Advancing swings arrive as large, fast candles with wide pullbacks.

Decision: Directional/expanding; same structure, different execution risk.

Opposite resolutionExample 3

Repeated boundary failures rotate through a stable midpoint.

Decision: Rotational; continuation assumptions are mismatched.

Near missExample 4

One large green candle erupts inside an established range.

Decision: Volatility expanded; directional structure is not yet established.

AmbiguousExample 5

Higher timeframe trends while execution timeframe compresses before a major event.

Decision: Conflicting job scales/event risk: no trade is defensible.

18–24 min · Unseen decision

Price remains inside a range but candle ranges triple after a scheduled announcement. Classification?

24–30 min · Deliberate practice

Classify ten hidden-edge 1h windows using exactly two labels: structure and volatility. Permit one playbook or write “none”. State one observation that would change each classification.

Save one pre-outcome screenshot and your classification. Ask your LLM teacher: “Which observation decided the classification? What would falsify it? Ignore the eventual profit.”
