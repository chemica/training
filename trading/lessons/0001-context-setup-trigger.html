Lesson 0001 · 30 minutes · One decision

Write a testable trade hypothesis.

By the end: Given a chart, write context, setup, trigger and invalidation without predicting the outcome.

Boundary: all charts and tickets are historical, replayed or paper-only. Judge the decision made with information available then—not the eventual P&L.

0–5 min · The invariant

A valid hypothesis names the current environment, a conditional opportunity, the observable event required to act, and the evidence that makes the idea wrong.

Context
Regime and structural location on the timeframe assigned to that job.
Setup
A conditional opportunity—not permission to enter.
Trigger
Observable evidence required before a paper entry.
Invalidation
Market evidence that contradicts the thesis, not merely a monetary stop.

Open the shared glossary if a supporting term is unfamiliar.

5–18 min · One structure, five outcomes

Read each chart left to right. The paths are schematics, not predictions; compare the decisive evidence rather than memorising their silhouettes.

CanonicalExample 1

4h advances; 1h pulls into a former breakout zone; 15m reclaims a minor swing.

Decision: Long is permitted only after the reclaim; acceptance below the zone invalidates.

Visual variationExample 2

The pullback is two large red candles rather than six small ones, but it holds the same protected swing.

Decision: Candle appearance changed; structural condition did not.

Opposite resolutionExample 3

The 15m briefly reclaims, then closes repeatedly below the higher-timeframe zone.

Decision: The continuation thesis is invalidated; do not relabel the loss as a better entry.

Near missExample 4

Price touches the zone and rallies without the written trigger.

Decision: No trade. A move without your trigger is not a missed valid execution.

AmbiguousExample 5

4h is advancing, but 1h overlaps around the zone and 15m has no directional close.

Decision: Unresolved; wait. Conflicting evidence is a classification.

18–24 min · Unseen decision

4h is ranging. Price reaches the upper boundary; 15m wicks above it and closes inside, but no lower high has formed. What is the best classification?

24–30 min · Deliberate practice

Choose one liquid BTC spot chart. Write exactly four lines—context, setup, trigger, invalidation—before revealing ten more candles. Score only whether every line was observable and fixed in advance.

Save one pre-outcome screenshot and your classification. Ask your LLM teacher: “Which observation decided the classification? What would falsify it? Ignore the eventual profit.”
